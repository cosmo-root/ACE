# ACE — Actually Comprehend Everything

I'm building a study harness. Early days, most of it is still undecided.

## The idea

Everybody already uses LLMs to help them study—getting explanations, asking
questions mid-study, and working through hard topics. ACE is a study harness
for what people already do. It is simply a cleaner, more focused implementation
of a use case millions of people already have.

Studying well is a solved problem. Learn at full depth. Prove it on real
problems. Come back after time passes and prove it again. Running that loop by
hand takes constant discipline, and overcomplicated tools get in the way and
perform worse. A good study harness stays focused on the work in front of you
without needing to swallow whole courses at once.

ACE runs the shape for you: a study plan with a memory that schedules the work,
checks the bar, and brings topics back on time. The method is standard. The
goal is a harness that holds you inside it, session after session.

## What it is

A record of understanding per topic, full-strength lessons, practice from real
textbooks and courses, a clear bar for passing each topic, and revisits after a
delay. I do the learning. The harness keeps the order and the standard steady.

The pieces I'm thinking about: a profile of what I know and how I learn best,
full-strength lessons, sourced problems with coaching, pass criteria per topic,
and spaced retests. Each piece is still TBD.
