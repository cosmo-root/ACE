# Calibration

How ACE decides what level and depth a lesson should have. Early notes; the mechanism is still open (see [Evaluation](evaluation.md)).

## What calibration means

ACE keeps a profile and pitches each lesson to fit: what you already know, how you learn best, and how deep the material should go.

## Open questions

- How is the learner profile built?
- How long is the diagnostic, and what does it ask?
- What is the target difficulty for practice?

## What the profile feeds

Calibration drives the lesson and the problem mix. The record of understanding per topic (see [Learning loop](learning-loop.md)) is what future calibration reads back.
